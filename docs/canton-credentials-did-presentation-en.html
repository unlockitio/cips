Credential model

One credential. Registration adds a separate lifecycle.

Credential and RegisteredCredential are views of the same contract, not competing identities.

Intrinsic contract

Credential

Holds the intrinsic credential data and validity. It can exist without a registry.

validUntil governs credential validity.

Optional registry view

RegisteredCredential

Exposes registration for that credential, when registration is chosen.

expiresAt governs registry retention.

One canonical contract; the registered view adds registry metadata. DSO paid retention would extend expiresAt, never validUntil. Payment and authorization for that extension remain open.

Identity and Canton authorization

The W3C issuer may be external. The anchor is on Canton.

Issuer

W3C identifier

The issuer identifier need not be a Canton Party. An external identifier does not become a Canton signatory.

Anchoring

Canton parties

Anchorers are mandatory Canton parties responsible for anchoring the credential.

Holding

Optional holders

Holders are optional Canton parties. The ledger contract remains Canton-specific.

Signatories: anchorers plus holders, deduplicated. Anchoring records a claim; it does not verify the claim. Encoding issuer or holder as a mandatory Canton Party in downstream templates would force migration or a false Party identity.

Subjects and claims

Who holds it? Who is it about?

Alice holds an ACME licence. Alice is the holder; ACME is the explicit credential subject.

Old key convention

Subject hidden in a key

licenseStatus!ACME identifies ACME by convention; bare licenseStatus implies Alice. This conflates claims and subject selection.

Draft model

Subject is explicit

Set the credential subject to ACME and use licenseStatus as the claim key. Holder Alice remains a separate role.

A compatibility projection for old key prefixes is a proposal, not implemented behavior. Do not infer the subject from a claim key in the new model.
Issuer, subject, holder and Canton anchorer are distinct roles.04 / 08

Credential lifecycle

Issue and renew. Register and retain separately.

Two clocks govern different things; neither requires a transaction merely because time passes.

Intrinsic lifecycle

Issue and renew

Factory issuance creates a credential. Renewal changes validUntil only: credential validity, not registry retention.

Registry lifecycle

Register, extend, archive

Registration is optional. Extension changes expiresAt only: registry retention, not credential validity. Archiving is separate.

Selected Daml lifecycle POC: compare these transitions. Offer / Accept, AddHolder, suspension, resumption and revocation remain open, not delivered. External off-ledger W3C proof verification is future work, not implemented in Daml.
Renewal and paid retention remain independent; extension payment and authorization are open (slide 2).06 / 08
